Research Objective
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evaluate the robustness of the Cross-Model Explanation Consistency Index (CECI) using bootstrap resampling of the unified permutation-SHAP consistency results.

Methodology
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset: NASA JM1
Models: RF, XGB, LGBM, MLP
Model pairs: 6
SHAP method: Unified permutation SHAP
Consistency dimensions: Global Spearman, Global Cosine, Local Spearman, Local Cosine
Normalization: (score + 1) / 2
Weighting: Equal weighting
Bootstrap repetitions: 5000
Confidence level: 95%
