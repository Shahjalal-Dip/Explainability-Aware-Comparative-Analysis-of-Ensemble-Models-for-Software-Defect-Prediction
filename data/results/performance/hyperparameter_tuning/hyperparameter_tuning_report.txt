Methodological Notes
--------------------
1. The locked 20% test set was never accessed.
2. SMOTE was applied only inside training folds.
3. Validation folds retained their original class distribution.
4. Hyperparameters were selected using development-set CV.
5. F1-score was used as the primary optimization criterion.
6. MCC, ROC-AUC and PR-AUC were retained as secondary measures.
