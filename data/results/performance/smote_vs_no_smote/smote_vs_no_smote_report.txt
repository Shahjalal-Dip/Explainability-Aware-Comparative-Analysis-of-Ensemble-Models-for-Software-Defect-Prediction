JM1 DEVELOPMENT-SET SMOTE VS NO-SMOTE COMPARISON
==========================================================================================

Experimental design
--------------------
Dataset: JM1 Development Set
Development set: 80% of original dataset
Test set: 20% - COMPLETELY UNTOUCHED
Validation: Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation
Comparison: SMOTE vs No-SMOTE
SMOTE applied: Training folds only
Random state: 42

Models
------
- Random Forest
- XGBoost
- LightGBM
- MLP
